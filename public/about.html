About BC CourseFinder™

BC CourseFinder™ is an AI-powered career guidance assistant designed specifically for South African Matric students interested in IT studies at Belgium Campus iTversity.

What is Belgium Campus?

Belgium Campus is a private higher education institution in Pretoria, South Africa, specialising exclusively in IT education. It is accredited by CHE and registered with DHET.

How the Bot Helps Students

  • ✅ Explains IT career paths (Software Developer, Data Scientist, Cybersecurity Analyst, etc.)
  • ✅ Clarifies subject requirements (Pure Maths, English, APS scores)
  • ✅ Compares qualifications: Degree vs Diploma vs Higher Certificate
  • ✅ Provides accurate entry requirements sourced from the official Belgium Campus website
  • ✅ Guides students to the official BC website for current fees, deadlines, and applications
  • ✅ Includes an APS Calculator to check programme eligibility
  • ✅ Saves chat history for registered users

Important Note

While this bot provides accurate information based on Belgium Campus's official materials, always verify critical details (application deadlines, fees, bursaries) on the official Belgium Campus website as they may change.
